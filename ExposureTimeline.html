Risk Brief

Payments feel instant, but approval, settlement, and final liability happen on different timelines. That gap is where risk lives.

The Mental Model

Payment processing is essentially a series of micro-loans.

The acquiring side releases funds with the expectation that each transaction will remain valid. Fraud, refunds and chargebacks can break that expectation later.

Clearing, Settlement, and Funding

Authorization

The sale is approved

The issuer confirms funds or credit are available.

Fulfillment

Goods are released

The merchant completes the sale before finality.

Settlement

Funds are deposited

The acquiring side advances money to the merchant.

After the Sale

Liability remains

Fraud, refunds, and chargebacks may reverse the funds.

Authorization is not a guarantee. It allows the transaction to proceed, but it does not eliminate the possibility of a future loss.
