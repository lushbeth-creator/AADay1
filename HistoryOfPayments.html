Payments didn't start with plastic.

Every control you'll learn about today, KYC, PCI, authorization, exists because of something that went wrong earlier in this timeline. Watch what happens with each new step up.

Long Ago

Trust was local, so was risk.

Early 1900s

Checks move money without cash in hand.

1950s

Diners Club launches the first charge card.

1960s–70s

Bank cards and the magnetic stripe arrive.

2000s–15

The EMV chip beats the stripe.

Today

The card is barely in the room, tap, wallet, or click.

Discuss

Look at that climb. What do you think kept getting added at each new step, convenience, or risk, or both?

Reflect

Notice the pattern: every new convenience in this timeline created a new kind of risk. That's the lens for the rest of today.
